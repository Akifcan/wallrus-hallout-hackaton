## What is DocScout?

DocScout is a next-generation research platform that helps users discover, organize, and transform information efficiently. Using advanced and specialized search techniques, the platform makes it easy to locate PDFs, slides, articles, and other academic resources across the web. Once retrieved, DocScout can summarize, rewrite, or create alternative versions of the content, turning raw data into actionable knowledge ready for study, analysis, or sharing.

Beyond simple discovery, DocScout acts as a comprehensive workspace for research management. Users can store notes, organize findings, link related documents, and generate insights with AI support. This integrated approach transforms scattered information into a coherent workflow, making research faster, more productive, and easier to collaborate on.

At the heart of DocScout is its connection to **Walrus**, a decentralized storage network on Sui. Walrus ensures that all research files, notes, and outputs are stored securely, tamper-proof, and censorship-resistant. By combining intelligent special search techniques, AI-powered content processing, and decentralized file storage, DocScout offers a robust and reliable environment for students, academics, and knowledge workers seeking an efficient and future-ready research solution.

## Features of DocScout

- **Advanced Document Search** – Find PDFs, slides, research papers, and academic content quickly with AI-enhanced search.

- **AI Summarization & Rewriting** – Automatically summarize long documents or generate alternative versions for easier understanding.

- **Content Organization** – Collect, categorize, and structure research notes and findings in one workspace.

- **Collaboration & Sharing** – Share curated materials and insights securely with peers or teams.

- **Walrus Storage Integration** – Store files on a decentralized, tamper-proof, censorship-resistant system powered by Sui blockchain.

- **Time-Saving Automation** – Automate repetitive tasks like summarization, document extraction, and note-taking.

- **Cross-Platform Access** – Access research files and summaries anytime, anywhere, with secure decentralized storage.

## Why We Built DocScout

We built DocScout to solve the inefficiency of traditional research workflows. Students and researchers waste too much time searching for materials and preparing them for use. DocScout streamlines this process by using AI to intelligently search, extract, summarize, and organize content, turning scattered information into structured, usable knowledge.

Research storage is another challenge, which is why DocScout integrates Walrus decentralized storage. Files, notes, and outputs are secure, tamper-proof, and permanently accessible, giving users confidence in their work. Overall, DocScout removes friction from research so users can focus on learning, analyzing, and creating.

## Problem

Finding and using research materials is slow, scattered, and inefficient. Students and researchers spend hours hunting for PDFs, slides, and academic documents, often struggling to locate reliable sources. Even after finding content, it usually requires summarizing, rewriting, or organizing before it can be useful. Traditional search engines aren't optimized for academic discovery, and existing tools fail to provide a complete workflow for collecting, processing, and storing research data. Centralized storage adds risk—documents can be lost, corrupted, or restricted without warning. This slows down research and reduces productivity.
